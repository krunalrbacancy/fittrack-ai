FitTrack AI Personal Nutrition & Training Plan

Weekly Goal: Reduce body fat while maintaining muscle mass through a moderate calorie deficit of roughly 500 kcal per day below maintenance.

Meal Timing Strategy: Eat within a 10-hour window each day, starting breakfast no earlier than 8am and finishing dinner by 6pm. This intermittent fasting approach has shown good results for appetite control during the first two weeks of the program. Avoid large meals within 3 hours of bedtime, as this has been linked to poorer sleep quality.

Protein Targets: Aim for at least 1.6 grams of protein per kilogram of bodyweight daily, spread across 3-4 meals. Good sources include chicken breast, paneer, eggs, Greek yogurt, lentils, and whey protein powder.

Supplement Recommendations: Take a multivitamin with breakfast. Consider a whey protein isolate supplement if daily protein intake falls below 80% of target on training days. Avoid pre-workout supplements containing more than 200mg of caffeine, since testing showed it caused sleep disruption when taken after 4pm.

Injury Notes: Left knee has shown mild discomfort during high-impact cardio such as running or jump rope. Recommended substitution: stationary cycling or swimming for cardio sessions until knee inflammation fully subsides. Only reintroduce running after two full weeks of pain-free low-impact training.

Sleep Guidance: Aim for 7-8 hours of sleep nightly. Poor sleep has been correlated with increased ghrelin (the hunger hormone), which can undermine adherence to a calorie deficit. Keep the bedroom cool and dark, and avoid screens for 30 minutes before bed.

Hydration: Drink at least 2.5 liters of water daily, more on training days or in hot weather. A simple check: urine should be pale yellow, not dark.

Weekly Training Split: Monday - upper body strength. Tuesday - low-impact cardio (cycling or swimming). Wednesday - lower body strength. Thursday - rest or light walking. Friday - full body strength. Saturday - low-impact cardio. Sunday - complete rest.

Emergency Protocol: In case of severe chest pain, dizziness, or fainting during exercise, stop immediately and contact emergency services. Do not attempt to push through symptoms that could indicate a cardiac event.

Progress Review: Re-measure weight and waist every Sunday morning before breakfast, under the same conditions each time for consistency. Expect roughly 0.3-0.5 kg of weight loss per week; faster loss may indicate the deficit is too aggressive and could risk muscle loss.
